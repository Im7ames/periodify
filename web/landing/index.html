How it works

Import when you choose

Sign in to Bromcom using your school’s Microsoft account and press Scrape to save the timetable or homework you’re viewing.

Repeat your teaching weeks

Set the number of weeks in your rotation, then capture each week in one session. Your saved timetable is available offline.

Check your wrist

Sync from the Android app to a paired Wear OS watch. Set bell times to see what’s on now and what’s coming up.

Get Periodify

Download the Android, Wear OS, Windows, macOS, or Linux package from GitHub. The web app and Linux can open a snapshot exported from another device.
